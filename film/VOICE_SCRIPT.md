# Voice script for the Emerald film

You read two parts: the farmer and the narrator. Emerald's voice is the live agent, so it answers your real recording. You don't voice it.

## How to record

- **Record it all in one go, as one file.** A phone voice memo is fine. Go somewhere quiet with soft furnishings, and hold the phone about a hand's width from your mouth.
- **Stay silent for 2 full seconds between lines.** That's how the lines get split automatically.
- **Read every line in the order below.** Don't read the IDs or the notes aloud.
- **If you fluff a line,** stop, wait 2 seconds, and carry on with the next line. Then tell me which line to fix, and record just that one again as its own file, named after its ID, such as `n07.m4a`.
- **Time needed:** about 5 minutes. The film has to stay under 3 minutes, so keep a natural, unhurried pace without long pauses inside a line.

Save the file as `D:\Projects\Agent Strands Hackathon\Project Emerald\data\voice\take.m4a` (any common audio format works), or just send it to me here.
